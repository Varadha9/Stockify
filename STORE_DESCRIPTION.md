Stockify is a modern, offline-first inventory management app built for small retail businesses, shop owners, and solo entrepreneurs. Manage your entire stock from your phone — no internet required, no subscription needed.

**📦 Inventory Management**
Add, edit, and delete inventory items with full details: name, category, SKU, price, quantity, supplier, description, and photo. Search and filter by category in real time. Sort by name, price, quantity, or low stock priority.

**📷 Barcode Scanner**
Scan any product barcode with your camera. The SKU field is auto-filled instantly — no manual typing needed.

**🔔 Low Stock Alerts**
Set a custom low-stock threshold for every item. Get a push notification when stock runs low, with a direct link to update quantities.

**📊 Reports & Analytics**
See your total inventory value, unit counts, and low-stock summary at a glance. Category and supplier breakdowns, a 7-day value trend chart, and a stock levels bar chart give you instant business insights.

**💾 Backup & Restore**
Export your full inventory to a CSV file and share it anywhere. Back up everything — inventory, activity logs, and snapshots — to a JSON file, and restore it anytime.

**🔐 Secure & Private**
All data stays on your device. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2 (never stored in plain text). No data is ever sent to any server.

**⚙️ Features at a glance**
• Add / Edit / Delete inventory items
• Barcode scanner (ZXing)
• Real-time search and category filters
• Bulk quantity adjustment
• Low stock push notifications
• Dashboard with 7-day value trend chart
• Reports: pie chart, bar chart, activity log
• CSV export and JSON backup / restore
• Multi-currency support (INR, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY)
• Fully offline — works without internet
• Change password from Settings
• Delete account and all data anytime

Built with ❤️ for small retail businesses.
